# Heartshard — Story Plot

Companion to the Full Game Plan · Laud Zion Cascalla

## The story in one paragraph

Three hundred years ago the Sunspire fell and its core shattered into thousands of Shards and seven whole **Heartshards**. A stranger from beyond Velmora, the **Wayfarer**, wakes with the seventh Heartshard, **the Mark**, fused into their palm: the only Heartshard that can absorb the others. The Dawn Covenant wants to seal the Heartshards away, the Umbral Court wants to wield them, and **Ezrim, the Last Keeper**, who caused the fall, wants to gather all seven and become the new heart of the world. The Wayfarer races all three across a broken continent and learns that the person who gave them the Mark helped bring the Sunspire down.

**Theme:** what do you do with power that isn't yours? Dawn hides it, Umbral uses it, Ezrim takes it. The Wayfarer has to find a fourth answer: share it.

## How the story is told

- **Inotia style, as in the original games:**
  - The story is a chapter-by-chapter journey with a clear hero, a small cast of companions and a boss at the end of every chapter.
  - Conversations use large character portraits beside a dialogue box, with the speaker's name and an expression change on key lines.
  - Each chapter opens with an illustrated title card and a short narrated intro, and closes with a cutscene played in-engine.
  - Town NPCs have their own small stories that change as the chapters progress.
  - Your hired mercenaries comment during the story, so the party feels like a group of people, not just helpers.
  - No voice acting at launch.
- **The Wayfarer** is a mostly silent hero with dialogue choices that set tone (kind, blunt, sly). Choices change lines and small rewards, not the main plot, except the faction choice.
- **Campaign chapters** run in private instances. Party members can join a friend's chapter and earn EXP, but the story progress belongs to the host.
- **The open world reflects the story:** Hollowed raids, roaming Shard beasts and faction patrols appear as world events in each zone after its chapter is finished.

## Prologue: The Marked

**Levels 1–10 · Harrowgate and its outskirts · teaches combat, mercenaries, trading and chat**

1. **The wreck.** The Wayfarer wakes beside a burning caravan on the road to Harrowgate. A glowing mark burns in their palm. Masked figures (the Hollowed) search the wreck, then flee when Ilsa Varn and her mercenaries ride in. *Tutorial: movement and basic attacks against two Hollowed scouts.*
2. **The Wayfarers' Hall.** Ilsa brings the Wayfarer to Harrowgate and, unimpressed, offers work instead of answers. *Tutorial: hire the first mercenary.*
3. **Pell's market.** Pell sells the Wayfarer a "genuine Sunspire relic" (a painted rock) and becomes their guide to town. *Tutorial: vending stalls, the trade window, chat channels.*
4. **Odd jobs.** Clearing Shard-touched pests from farms outside the walls. The Mark pulses near every Shard. *Tutorial: quests, loot, equipping visible gear.*
5. **The gate.** At night, Shard-swollen beasts led by **the Gatebreaker**, a boar the size of a cart, smash into Harrowgate. The Wayfarer fights beside the guard.
6. **Field boss: the Gatebreaker.** When it falls, the Mark pulls every Shard out of the beasts in a burst of light, in front of the whole town.
7. **Two visitors.** The next morning, Ser Calden of the Dawn and Sable of the Umbral arrive within an hour of each other, both asking for "the Marked." Teodric the Lampwright quietly asks the Wayfarer to visit his workshop.

**Ending hook:** Teodric looks at the Mark and whispers, "So it chose you." He won't explain yet.

## Chapter 1: Glass and Ash

**Levels 10–25 · Ashen Dunes · Sunken Reliquary · introduces parties, refining, sigils, travel mounts at 20**

1. **The legend.** Teodric tells the story of the seven Heartshards and admits the Mark is one of them. He sends the Wayfarer to the Ashen Dunes, where the Ash Clans have sent word of "a queen made of glass."
2. **Two escorts.** Calden and Sable both insist on coming. Their bickering is the chapter's running joke, and their side quests show their values: Calden protects caravans by the rules, Sable bribes a smuggler to get the same result faster.
3. **The Ash Clans.** The Clans live by bending Shard twisting into curses (the Hexer origin). Their elder, **Mother Keshra**, distrusts outsiders. The Wayfarer earns trust by clearing Hollowed camps poisoning the wells. *Side story: a Clan child twisted by a Shard; the Wayfarer can help the Clan's Hexers treat her.*
4. **Travel mount (level 20).** The Clans gift a dune strider for crossing the deep sands. *Unlocks travel mounts.*
5. **The hive.** The **Glass Hive** grows around a buried temple, the Sunken Reliquary. Its queen, **Saqqra**, swallowed the Glass Heartshard long ago, and her hive turned the grassland into the dunes.
6. **The race.** A Hollowed war party breaches the Reliquary at the same time. Their leader, **Vessa of the Hollowed**, is polite and sad: "You'll understand when he explains it."
7. **Dungeon boss: Queen Saqqra.** Phases: glass spikes that shatter into hazards, summoned drones, then a frenzy when Vessa's team attacks the queen too. *Party content: recommended group of 3–4, mercenaries can fill.*
8. **The first Heartshard.** Saqqra falls. Both the Wayfarer and Vessa reach for the Glass Heartshard, but the Mark pulls it in. The Wayfarer's power surges (a story reason for the level jump), and Vessa escapes.

**Twist:** Vessa calls the Wayfarer "Teodric's gift." The Wayfarer returns to Harrowgate with questions.

## Chapter 2: The Rootbound Choice

**Levels 25–40 · Whisperwood · Rootdeep Hollow · introduces the faction choice at 30, guilds, pets, job advancement at 40**

1. **Teodric dodges.** He claims Vessa is lying and sends the Wayfarer to Whisperwood, where the forest has started killing travelers. Ilsa notices he is afraid.
2. **The Wardens.** The Warden rangers (the Warden class origin) explain that the forest's guardian, **the Thornmother**, has guarded the Thorn Heartshard for 300 years and is now sick. *Side story: taming a wounded forest beast, which unlocks pets.*
3. **Poison in the roots.** The Wayfarer traces the sickness to Hollowed priests feeding the Thornmother corrupted Shards to weaken her grip on the Heartshard.
4. **Pell follows.** Pell sneaks along and is caught by Hollowed recruiters who show him a cured Shard-twisted man. Pell reveals his own twisted arm. The Wayfarer talks him home, or doesn't; either way he returns to Harrowgate shaken. *This plants the seed that the Hollowed are not simple villains.*
5. **The choice (level 30).** Aurelle and Morvaine arrive in person. The Thorn Heartshard is too wild for the Mark to absorb yet, so someone must hold it once it is freed.
   - **Dawn path:** escort Dawn sealers through the forest, defend the sealing ritual, learn the Dawn's story of the fall. Calden becomes the player's companion for the chapter.
   - **Umbral path:** sabotage Hollowed camps, bargain with the forest's spirits, learn the Umbral's story of the fall. Sable becomes the companion.
   - Each path has its own quests, rewards and cosmetics. The choice is permanent and sets the faction for faction content.
6. **Dungeon boss: the Thornmother.** Inside Rootdeep Hollow, the poisoned Thornmother fights to protect the Heartshard from everyone. Phases: root prisons, spore clouds, then a clear phase where she briefly recovers and stops attacking. The player must cleanse the roots rather than just deal damage.
7. **Her last words.** Free of the poison, she gives the Heartshard to the chosen faction and dies saying one name: **Ezrim**.
8. **The theft.** Days later, the Hollowed raid whichever faction holds the Thorn Heartshard (the Dawn's sealing vault or the Umbral's binding forge) and take it. Neither plan could protect it.
9. **Job advancement (level 40).** Class mentors offer the advanced paths. In story terms, the Wayfarer is now strong enough that both faction leaders start to worry.

**Twist:** in the ruins of the raid, a note in Vessa's hand: "Ask Teodric who held the lamp."

## Chapter 3: The Rime Below the Spire

**Levels 40–60 · Frostveil Peaks · Hoarfrost Vault · introduces battle mounts at 45 and open-world bosses**

1. **Teodric's confession.** Confronted, Teodric admits everything: he and Ezrim were acolytes of the Sunspire. He held the ritual lamp while Ezrim tried to become the core. When it went wrong, Teodric caught the seventh Heartshard and has hidden it for 300 years, kept alive by a Shard burning in his lamp. He put the Mark into a stranger from beyond Velmora because outsiders are not twisted by any region's Shards. He paid Ilsa to bring them to Harrowgate. *Dialogue choice: forgive, condemn or stay silent. It changes Teodric's lines later, not the plot.*
2. **Ilsa's admission.** Ilsa admits she knew she was carrying someone important, not why. She lost her whole crew to the Hollowed in that ambush. Her loyalty to the Wayfarer becomes personal.
3. **The frozen peaks.** The Frostveil riders, a mountain people who fight mounted, guide the Wayfarer into the peaks frozen by the Rime Heartshard.
4. **Battle mounts (level 45).** The riders test the Wayfarer and grant a battle mount. *Unlocks battle mounts.*
5. **Both leaders turn.** Aurelle orders Calden to bring the Wayfarer to the Dawn vault to be sealed away with the Mark. Morvaine orders Sable to cut the Mark from the Wayfarer's hand. Both companions refuse, and both factions' agents hunt the Wayfarer through the peaks. *This happens on both faction paths; the faction the player chose is the one that feels most like a betrayal.*
6. **Calden and Sable.** Forced together, the knight and the spy argue their way into a real friendship. *Optional campfire scene with dialogue choices.*
7. **The Hoarfrost Vault.** Ezrim has gathered the Thorn and Rime Heartshards under the ice. Vessa meets the Wayfarer at the gate and asks them, honestly, to listen to Ezrim before fighting.
8. **Dungeon boss: Varkhal the Rimebound.** Ezrim's war construct, powered by the Rime Heartshard. Phases: freezing floor, ice pillars, a mounted phase on the vault bridge.
9. **Ezrim.** He appears for the first time: calm, polite and kind. He explains his plan: rebuilt with him at its core, the Sunspire would pull every Shard back out of the world. Everyone twisted by Shards (the Hollowed, Morvaine, Pell, the Ash Clans' children) would be cured. He leaves out the cost: everyone whose life depends on Shards, including Teodric, would die, and he would rule what remains.
10. **The theft of glass.** Ezrim tears the Glass Heartshard out of the Mark. The Wayfarer survives only because Teodric burns his own lamp to shield them. Ezrim escapes into **the Scar** with three Heartshards.

**Launch ending:** Teodric survives, but weak and aging fast. Aurelle and Morvaine, both shamed by their agents' refusal, agree to a truce at the Wayfarers' Hall. The Wayfarer is not a Dawn tool or an Umbral weapon anymore; they lead the hunt. The final shot: the Scar glowing on the horizon, and three lights still lost somewhere in Velmora.

## Seasons after launch

Each season adds a region, the hunt for one lost Heartshard, a dungeon, open-world bosses and a season pass themed on its story.

| Season                       | Region                                      | Lost Heartshard      | Story                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1: The Drowned Coast**     | Brinehollow, a sunken coastline             | **Tide Heartshard**  | The truce's first joint mission. Morvaine secretly hopes the Tide Heartshard can cure her face, and nearly breaks the truce for it.                                                                                                     |
| **2: Embers of Cinderreach** | Cinderreach, a volcanic region              | **Ember Heartshard** | Vessa begins to doubt Ezrim after seeing what he did to the Hollowed who failed him. Pell faces the Hollowed recruiters again.                                                                                                          |
| **3: The Long Dusk**         | Gloamhold, a city stuck in endless twilight | **Dusk Heartshard**  | The Dusk Heartshard shows the past: the Wayfarer sees the real night of the fall. Teodric's lamp finally goes out.                                                                                                                      |
| **4: The Scar**              | The crater of the Sunspire                  | All seven            | The final battle for the Heartshards. The Wayfarer's answer to the theme: the Heartshards are not hidden, used or taken, but returned to the regions that grew up around them. Ezrim's fate is decided by the player's earlier choices. |

Each season ends with the Hollowed winning or losing that Heartshard depending on the season's server-wide event results, so the whole server shares the outcome.

## Consistency rules for writers

- There are exactly **seven** Heartshards: the Mark, Glass, Thorn, Rime, Tide, Ember, Dusk. Never add an eighth.
- Heartshards are story objects. They never drop, sell or trade. Ordinary Shards, Shard dust and sigils are the game items.
- Neither faction is evil. Every Dawn and Umbral scene should show a reason to agree with them.
- The Hollowed are people who want a cure, not monsters. Name them, give them letters, let some of them leave.
- Ezrim never gloats, shouts or lies outright. He leaves things out.
- Level ranges, unlocks and zone names must match the Full Game Plan.
- Follow the content rules: plain language and no dashes as punctuation in any text players see.
